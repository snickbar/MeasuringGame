Size Em Up! is a real-time multiplayer party game about guessing scale. Every round you're shown two things - like ants and a watermelon, or a coin and a car - and you guess how many of the small one it takes to match the big one. Closest guess wins the round. No hard math, just gut instinct and a little friendly chaos.

Gather your friends, family, or coworkers on one room code and play right in your browser - no downloads, no accounts, no sign-up required.

WHAT MAKES IT FUN
- Guess-the-scale gameplay that's easy to explain in 10 seconds and hard to stop playing
- Real-time multiplayer for up to 10 players in a room, all guessing at once
- Second-chance scoring: after guesses lock, pick someone else's answer or double down on your own for bonus points
- Bots (Easy/Medium/Hard) to practice solo or fill out a small group
- Reactions, streak call-outs, and a "Spot On!" / "Perfect!" moment when you nail the number
- Customizable rounds, timer, and item pairing (keep sizes close, or let anything go)

AVATAR SHOP
Every point you earn becomes a coin when the game ends, win or lose. Spend coins in the Avatar Shop to pull a random new avatar - Common, Rare, or Ultra-rare - and grow your collection of unlockable characters.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Create a room and share the 4-letter code with your group
2. Everyone picks a name and avatar and joins the lobby
3. Each round, guess how many of the small item fit inside the big one
4. Closest guess to the real answer scores the points

Perfect for game night, long car rides, or killing time in a group chat. Size Em Up! runs entirely in your mobile browser, so there's nothing to install to get started.
