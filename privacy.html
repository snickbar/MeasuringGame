Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 (revised)

Size Em Up! is a real-time multiplayer party game about guessing sizes and quantities. This page explains what information the game collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you create or join a room, you choose:

  • A display name (you can type anything — it doesn't need to be your real name)
  • An avatar from a built-in set of illustrated icons

While you play, the game also stores your guesses, scores, and any emoji reactions you send, so the game can run and other players in your room can see them.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, real names, phone numbers, payment information, precise location, contacts, or photos. There are no user accounts — anyone can play without signing up.

Information stored only on your device

To make repeat visits more convenient, your browser saves a few small preferences locally (using standard browser storage), including your last-used name and avatar, your sound on/off setting, the code of your most recent room, and whether you've already seen the how-to-play tips. This stays on your device — it isn't sent anywhere except when you use it to join a game.

Your device also stores a randomly generated identifier used only for the coin/avatar-unlock feature described below.

How your information is used

Your name, avatar, guesses, and scores are used solely to run the game you're playing — showing other players in your room who you are and how you're doing. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold to anyone.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Game data is stored using Google Firebase (a cloud database service), scoped to the specific room code you're playing in. Rooms are automatically deleted a short time after everyone leaves — the game doesn't keep a permanent history of past games or players.

Coins and unlockable avatars

Size Em Up! has an optional coin system: your score from a finished game is converted into coins, which can be spent to unlock additional avatar options. To make this possible, your device is given a randomly generated ID (stored locally, not tied to your name or any personal information), and your coin balance and unlocked avatars are saved under that ID so they carry over between games. This is tied to your specific device and browser — clearing your browser's site data or switching devices will reset it, since there's no account system to restore it from.

Third-party services

The game runs on Google Firebase for hosting and its real-time database. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Size Em Up! does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services of its own.

Children's privacy

Size Em Up! is a family-friendly party game, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no real names or accounts are required to play, we believe the risk here is low — but if you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can clear the preferences saved on your device at any time through your browser's site-settings or "clear browsing data" option. Leaving a room removes you from it, and the room's data is deleted automatically once it's empty.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
