Match its cute rounded cartoon style, warm dark outlines, gray fur, huge pink ears, cream belly, pink feet and tail. In each cell show the SAME complete small mouse in side three-quarter profile facing RIGHT, walking upright on hind feet while holding a little triangular yellow Swiss cheese wedge in both front paws against its belly. Four successive walk-cycle poses: left foot forward/right back, passing feet, right foot forward/left back, passing feet opposite. Clearly articulated bent knees and alternating feet, grounded heel/toe motion, subtle body bob. Keep identical character size/head/body/cheese, stable eye line, equal baseline, full tail inside each cell, generous transparent gutters between cells. No labels, no dividing lines, no floor, no shadow, no scenery. True transparent background. This is one production walk-cycle sprite sheet; no other art.
