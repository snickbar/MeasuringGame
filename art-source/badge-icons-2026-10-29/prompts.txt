Badge Art Prompts
11 prompts, 42 new badge icons. Each prompt makes 4 different icons on one sheet, so cut them apart after (the file names are listed under each title).

Settings that help: keep them small (about 256 pixels per icon is plenty). The game draws the gold, silver or bronze medal itself, so these are just the picture in the middle. If two icons come out too alike, regenerate only that prompt.
