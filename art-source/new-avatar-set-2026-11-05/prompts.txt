New Avatar Prompts
12 new avatars, one prompt each. Every prompt starts by telling the image maker to generate a picture.

Attach 3 or 4 of your existing avatars as the style references each time. Ask for one image per prompt; grids tended to come out with duplicates.

Big Robot Suit
mech suit with a tiny pilot
Copy prompt
Generate an image. Create ONE transparent PNG avatar for a mobile game. Match the attached STYLE REFERENCES, the same mobile game's avatars: simple friendly 2D cartoon illustration with warm dark-brown outlines, flat colors with restrained soft cel shading, small simple dark oval or dot eyes (NOT big anime eyes), rounded proportions, clean silhouette. Transparent background, centered square image with about 8% transparent padding on all sides, the whole subject visible. No text, no badge, no scenery and no extra props except the ones named. Not photorealistic, not 3D. One subject only. It must be a distinct new character, not a recolor of the references. The subject: A big, chunky, friendly robot suit (a mech) shown from the chest up, facing the viewer. Steel-blue and orange armor plates, round rivets, wide shoulders with small pistons, a short square head with a glowing yellow visor. A small round glass cockpit dome in the chest shows a tiny smiling pilot waving. Gentle smile painted on the faceplate.
