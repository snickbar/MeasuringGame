Mascot prompts
Banner, tutorial and cheer art for the new mode mascots. Every prompt starts by telling the image maker to generate a picture, and the copy button copies the whole prompt.

How to use these
For each mascot, make the banner picture first. Attach it as a reference when you run the tutorial and cheer prompts so the character stays the same.
Every prompt asks for a plain white background. I cut them out, as with the objects.
Poses for results screens (like "oops") can come later, one at a time, when we get to those screens.
High/Low Survival (bird and shark), Survival (knight and dragon) and Which Is Bigger (elephant and mouse) already have art, so they are not in this list.

Our existing red cartoon ant mascot (attach the current ant picture as a reference): a friendly red ant with big round eyes, a wide smile and two curly antennae, standing upright on two legs.
Banner
Generate an image of a friendly red cartoon ant (matching our existing ant mascot) standing upright, wearing a small striped party hat, holding a party popper in one hand with a puff of confetti bursting out, waving with the other hand. Wide landscape picture, about 3:2, with a little empty space around the character. Style: glossy cartoon sticker art in the same style as our game avatars, with thick dark-brown outlines, bright saturated colors, soft gradient shading and shiny highlights, friendly for all ages. The whole character is visible and nothing is cropped. Plain solid white background, no ground shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no border.
