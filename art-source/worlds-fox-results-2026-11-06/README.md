# Worlds results: fox in the balloon

Three selected PNGs: cheering when the meter rises, calm when it stays about the same, and worried when it falls. Every image shows the complete balloon, suspension ropes, fox and wicker basket on a plain white background. All canvases are the same 3:4 portrait dimensions.

The existing aviator-fox banner was attached to every generation. The current hot-air-balloon illustration supplied the envelope reference for the initial calm version; the calm full-balloon composition then anchored the cheering and worried poses. This preserves the fox, balloon shape, stripe layout, basket and overall framing across the three images. The worried balloon has reduced gloss as requested.

Open `index.html` for the gallery or `review.jpg` for the three-pose sheet. `manifest.json` includes the user's prompts, reference paths and selected final filenames. `fox-balloon-worried-v2.png` corrects an extra paw and supersedes the earlier worried attempt; the final ZIP excludes that earlier attempt.

No clouds, sky, ground, sandbags or cargo were added. Cloud drift, vertical movement and sparkles are left for the user's later animation work. These images are not integrated into the game's results screens yet.

Rebuild dimension checks and the review gallery with `node scripts/build-fox-results-review.cjs`.
